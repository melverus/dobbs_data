# dobbs_data
Analysis of maternal mortality and other adverse outcomes since the overturning of Roe v Wade

## Data Sources
 
- **CDC WONDER** — [wonder.cdc.gov](https://wonder.cdc.gov)
  - *Natality* dataset: live births by state, race/ethnicity, and year
  - *Underlying Cause of Death* dataset: maternal deaths by state, race/ethnicity, and year
  - Years used: 2018–2023 (pre- and post-Dobbs)
- **Guttmacher Institute** — [guttmacher.org](https://www.guttmacher.org)
  - State-level abortion policy classifications, used to group states into "restrictive" vs. "protective" buckets
